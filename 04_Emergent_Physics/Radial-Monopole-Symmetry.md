# Radial Monopole Symmetry

## 1. Physical Premise & Context
After ensemble coarse-graining ([[Ensemble-Averaging-Observation]]), anisotropic lattice contributions cancel, leaving a strictly monopolar (spherically symmetric) source term. This enforces the inverse-square law for emergent long-range forces and fixes the radial structure of $G_{\text{eff}}$ (see [[Effective-G-Coupling]]).

## 2. Mathematical Formulation
$$
\langle F \rangle(r) = \frac{G_{\text{eff}}\, m_1 m_2}{r^2}\,\hat{r}, \qquad \nabla\cdot\mathbf{g} = -4\pi G_{\text{eff}}\,\rho
$$
