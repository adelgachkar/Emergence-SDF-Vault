# Asymptotic Tangential MOND Transition

In the SDF variational framework, Modified Newtonian Dynamics (MOND) behavior emerges asymptotically at galactic scales without postulating cold dark matter particles:

$$a_0 \equiv \frac{c_{\text{eff}}}{\tau_d} \gamma_B \approx 1.2 \times 10^{-10} \, \text{m/s}^2$$

Where:
* $c_{\text{eff}}$ is the emergent speed of light from [[Time-as-Residual-Rearrangement-Debt]]
* $\tau_d$ is the macro rearrangement delay from [[Hysteresis-Cost-TimeDelay]]
* $\gamma_B$ is the Berry-phase geometric coupling factor

In the weak-acceleration limit ($a_N \ll a_0$), the effective gravitational acceleration transitions to:

$$\|\nabla \Phi_{\text{eff}}\| = \sqrt{\|\nabla \Phi_N\| \cdot a_0}$$

This transition corresponds to the macroscopic limit where the physical rearrangement debt of the lattice saturates, forcing the tangential velocity curves of virialized structures to remain flat:

$$v_{\text{flat}} = \sqrt[4]{G M a_0}$$
