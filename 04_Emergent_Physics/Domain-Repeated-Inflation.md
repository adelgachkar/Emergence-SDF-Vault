# Domain-Repeated-Inflation

## 1. Domino Boundary Expansion
When localized reactive energy deficit accumulates at boundary bottlenecks, the strain cannot relax locally and propagates via domino-like edge-bias transitions:

$$\nabla \cdot \mathbf{v}_{\text{inflation}} = \Omega_{\text{rec}} = \sum_{\langle ij \rangle} b_{ij} (\dot{\phi}_i - \dot{\phi}_j)$$

Where $b_{ij} \neq 0$ breaks spatial isotropy, converting symmetric bubble expansion into **directional strain and collective polarization**.

## 2. Coalescence into Polarized Macro-Cavities
As neighboring domains mutually compensate for their radiative leakage, individual boundary walls coalesce:
1. Individual void boundaries merge along minimal action surfaces.
2. Polarization vector aligns globally: $\mathbf{P}_{\text{macro}} = \frac{1}{V} \int_{\mathcal{V}} \mathbf{P}_{\text{eff}}(\mathbf{x}) d^3x \neq 0$.
3. Macro-domains establish kinetic stability against high-frequency decay.
