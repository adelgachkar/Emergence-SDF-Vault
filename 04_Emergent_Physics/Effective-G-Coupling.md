# Effective G Coupling

## 1. G as a Derived Property
In the Emergence-SDF framework, $G$ is not a fundamental constant of the universe. It is a **coupling strength** proportional to the substrate's impedance $Z$ and the latency $\tau_d$ of the reactive dark modes:
$$G_{\text{eff}} \approx \frac{\ell_{\text{Planck}}^2 \cdot c_{\text{eff}}^3}{\hbar} \cdot \left( \frac{\tau_d}{\tau_{\text{cycle}}} \right)$$

## 2. Dependence on Hysteresis
The strength of effective gravitational interaction is strictly determined by the energy loss (hysteresis cost) per cycle within the frustrated tetrahedral loops. High hysteresis latency ($\tau_d$) increases the "stiffness" of the emergent metric, manifesting as a higher effective gravitational coupling.

## 3. Scaling Laws
This explains why $G_{\text{eff}}$ appears constant at galactic scales but may show scale-dependent variations in highly dense or non-equilibrium regimes (the key to explaining MOND-like effects).
