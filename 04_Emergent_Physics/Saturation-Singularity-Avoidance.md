# Saturation & Singularity Avoidance

## 1. Physical Premise & Context
The statistical void limit ([[Statistical-Void-Limit]]) imposes a finite exclusion-sphere packing bound. As local density approaches saturation, further compaction is blocked by lattice exclusion, preventing curvature divergence and replacing classical singularities with a saturated plateau.

## 2. Mathematical Formulation
$$
\rho(r) \le \rho_{\text{sat}} = \frac{N_{\max}}{V_{\text{excl}}}, \qquad \lim_{r\to 0} \langle \rho \rangle = \rho_{\text{sat}} < \infty
$$
