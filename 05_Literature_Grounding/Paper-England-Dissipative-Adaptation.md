# Literature Grounding: England's Dissipation-Driven Adaptation

## 1. Key Conceptual Anchors

* **Absorption-history bias:** In externally driven, thermodynamically absorbing matter, configurations that have historically absorbed and dissipated more work are exponentially favored in subsequent dynamics — matter self-organizes toward better *absorbers* without any fitness function (England, *Nat. Nanotech.* **10**, 919 (2015), DOI: 10.1038/nnano.2015.250).
* **Statistical formalization:** Perunov, Marsland & England (*Phys. Rev. X* **6**, 021036 (2016), DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevX.6.021036) derive the irreversibility-based weight: driven matter adopts states with special histories of extra heat emission.
* **Rare-event rearrangements:** In driven glasses and amorphous systems, relaxation proceeds by rare, avalanche-like rearrangement events that reset accumulated local stress — the substrate "remembers" its loading history in its defect structure.

## 2. Cross-Vault Mappings to SDF Framework

* [[Time-as-Residual-Rearrangement-Debt]]: SDF's "accumulative fracture with residual bed" — phase debt building until a rare rearrangement settles it — matches the driven-glass picture of stress accumulation punctuated by avalanches; SDF generalizes the accounting from mechanical stress to Berry-phase debt.
* [[Hysteresis-Cost-TimeDelay]]: the hysteresis cost per cycle is the SDF version of England's absorbed-work ledger; the delay bound plays the role of the relaxation-time bottleneck that makes events rare.
* [[Macro-Network-Action-Optimization]]: SDF's "minimization of total reactive debt" is structurally the selection rule of dissipative adaptation (favor configurations that have dissipated more), restated variationally.
* [[Noise-Quenching-Homeostasis]]: amplitude-dependent damping as homeostat parallels how driven matter self-stabilizes at absorbing configurations.

## 3. Formal Bridge & Divergences

**Bridge:** both frameworks make *irreversible dissipation history* — not energy minimization alone — the selector of stable structure; SDF's stationary-action closure can be read as a variational restatement of the England–Perunov weight. **Divergence:** England's results are asymmetric-time fluctuation theorems about driven reservoirs; SDF claims the same selection for an *undriven* self-closing lattice. SDF should either introduce the drive explicitly (the vacuum foam as reservoir) or prove the closure selects the same states without one.
