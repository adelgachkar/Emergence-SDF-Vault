# Literature Grounding: MIT 22.02 — Coherent Neutron Scattering & Lattice Correlation

## 1. Key Conceptual Anchors
* **Fermi Pseudopotential & Scattering Lengths:**
  $$V(\mathbf{r}) = \frac{2\pi\hbar^2}{m} \sum_j b_j \delta(\mathbf{r} - \mathbf{R}_j)$$
  Decomposition of total scattering into coherent ($b_c$) and incoherent/self ($b_i$) cross sections: $\overline{b^2} = b_c^2 + b_i^2$.
* **Dynamic Structure Factor $S(\mathbf{q}, \omega)$:**
  $$\frac{d^2\sigma}{d\Omega d\omega} = N \frac{k_f}{k_i} \left[ b_i^2 S_{\text{self}}(\mathbf{q}, \omega) + b_c^2 S(\mathbf{q}, \omega) \right]$$
  Direct measure of space-time density-density correlation functions in discrete lattices.
