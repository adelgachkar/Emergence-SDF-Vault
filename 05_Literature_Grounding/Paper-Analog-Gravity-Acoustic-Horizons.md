# Literature Grounding: Analog Gravity & Acoustic Horizons

## 1. Key Conceptual Anchors

* **Emergent metric from a medium:** Long-wavelength perturbations of a moving fluid (or BEC) propagate on an *effective Lorentzian metric* whose light cones are set by the medium's sound speed — gravity-like kinematics without fundamental spacetime curvature (Barceló, Liberati & Visser, *Living Rev. Relativity* **8**, 12 (2005), DOI: 10.12942/lrr-2005-12; Visser, *Acoustic Black Holes*, 1998).
* **Horizon as a velocity threshold:** When the flow locally exceeds the internal wave speed ($|v| > c_s$), an **acoustic horizon** forms: outward modes can no longer propagate against the flow, and one side of the medium becomes causally disconnected — trapping phonons exactly as a gravitational horizon traps light.
* **Hawking radiation in the lab:** Steinhauer's BEC experiments observed self-amplifying Hawking-like correlations across such horizons (e.g., *Nat. Phys.* **12**, 959 (2016), DOI: 10.1038/nphys3683).

## 2. Cross-Vault Mappings to SDF Framework

* [[Pre-Friedmann-Cosmological-Closure]]: the SDF horizon condition $v_{\text{inflation}} = c_{\text{eff}} = \ell^*/\tau_d$ is the direct analog of the acoustic threshold $|v| = c_s$: the causal boundary forms where domain expansion saturates the lattice's wave speed. SDF's contribution is deriving $c_{\text{eff}}$ from substrate rearrangement latency rather than a material sound speed.
* [[Metric-Tensor-Emergence]]: the tetrad-impedance construction ($g_{\mu\nu} = e_\mu^a e_\nu^b \eta_{ab}$ from network impedance) is the SDF counterpart of the acoustic-metric derivation $g_{\mu\nu} \propto \rho/c_s$ from fluid parameters; both make the metric a property of the medium, not of spacetime.
* [[Saturation-Singularity-Avoidance]]: capacity saturation as the compensatory mechanism parallels condensate depletion/sonic-point regularization, which replaces singularities by finite-density plateaus.
* [[Domain-Repeated-Inflation]]: transonic domain transition (subsonic → supersonic patches) is the established picture of horizon nucleation that SDF's domino expansion reuses.

## 3. Formal Bridge & Divergences

**Bridge:** analog gravity has a rigorous derivation chain (hydrodynamic expansion → effective metric → horizon → Hawking spectrum); SDF's claim "horizon = saturation of accumulation capacity by phase shift" matches the threshold step and inherits its consistency. **Divergence:** analog gravity does not claim the medium *is* gravity — it is a simulator. For SDF to be physics rather than analogy, the impedance-derived metric must reproduce a universal coupling (Newtonian limit, [[Effective-G-Coupling]]) and the quasi-normal mode spectrum of real horizons — both currently stated, not derived.
