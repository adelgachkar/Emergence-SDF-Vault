# Pentagonal Frustration Numerical Coupling

This note provides the rigorous numerical values and parameters corresponding to the pentagonal geometric frustration and phase holonomy across tetrahedral lattice clusterings.

## 1. Geometric Frustration & Gap Constants

The packing defect resulting from five regular tetrahedra sharing a single common hinge edge is parameterized by:

- **Hinge Angular Deficit**:
  $$\delta_\theta = 2\pi - 5\arccos(1/3) \approx 7.356103^\circ \approx 0.128388\text{ rad}$$
- **Induced Geometric Berry Phase**:
  $$\gamma_B \approx 0.0605\text{ rad}$$
- **Cumulative Angular Deviation**:
  $$\Theta_{\text{cum}} = 5 \cdot \theta_d = 5 \arccos(1/3) \approx 352.643895^\circ$$

## 2. Regularization & Numerical Stability

- **Singularity Avoidance Radius**:

- **Transfer Matrix Spectral Radius**:
  $$\lambda_{\max}(T) \approx 1.0$$
- **Screening Correlation Length**:

## 3. Deficit Disambiguation (canonical)

Three distinct "geometric defects" must not be conflated; only the third is the SDF deficit:

| Quantity | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Interior-angle sum excess of a pentagon ($5\times180^\circ-360^\circ$) | $180^\circ$ | ❌ **retired** — a property of the polygon in isolation, not of the packing |
| 2D tiling deficit at a vertex ($360^\circ-3\times108^\circ$) | $36^\circ$ | ❌ planar analogue, not the 3D packing defect |
| **3D packing deficit: $2\pi - 5\arccos(1/3)$** | **$7.356103^\circ = 0.128388$ rad** | ✅ canonical (this note) |

The deficit is inevitable for five *regular* tetrahedra about a common edge in flat space ($5\arccos(1/3) < 2\pi$ strictly); the three classical escapes are distortion (Frank–Kasper), curvature (600-cell closes it exactly on $S^3$), and disclination lines. SDF selects a fourth channel: conversion to $U(1)$ holonomy ([[Emergent-Berry-Gauge-Lagrangian]]).

## 4. Void Cavity and Neck Geometry

For tangent spheres $r_0 = a/2$ at tetrahedron vertices:

- **Void inscribed sphere**: $r_{\text{in}} = r_0(\sqrt{3/2}-1) \approx 0.1124\,a$
- **Neck inradius** (triangular window): $\rho = r_0(2/\sqrt3-1) \approx 0.0774\,a$
- **Adjacent-void center spacing**: $d_{\text{cc}} = r_0\sqrt{2/3} \approx 0.408\,a$
- **Void volume**: $V_{\text{void}} = \tfrac43\pi r_0^3 - \tfrac{8\sqrt2}{3}r_0^3 \approx 0.4176\,r_0^3$ — consistent with [[Statistical-Void-Limit]].
