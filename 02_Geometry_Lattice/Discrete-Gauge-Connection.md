# Discrete Gauge Connection

## 1. Edge Phase Formulation
Transporting an excitation state between nodes $i$ and $j$ involves a unitary transformation $U_{ij}$, representing the local reactive phase vector:
$$U_{ij} = \exp\left( i \int_{\mathbf{x}_i}^{\mathbf{x}_j} \mathbf{A}_{\text{micro}} \cdot d\mathbf{l} \right)$$
where $\mathbf{A}_{\text{micro}}$ is the local reactive potential generated by the tetrahedral frustration.

## 2. Holonomy and Curvature
The pentagonal loop frustration (geometric frustration) acts as a Wilson loop that does not close to identity:
$$\mathcal{W}_{\mathcal{C}_5} = \prod_{\text{loop}} U_{ij} = e^{i \Phi_{\text{Berry}}} \neq 1$$
This non-zero holonomy is the discrete precursor to the field strength tensor $F_{\mu\nu}$ in the continuum limit.

## 3. Emergence
As the lattice density $N \to \infty$, the accumulation of these phase factors creates the emergent macroscopic vector potential $\mathbf{A}_{\mu}$, bridging the gap from pure geometry to electrodynamics and gravity.
