# Bandgap Phase Recycling and Dark Modes

## 1. The Leakage Recycling Theorem
In conventional open resonators, boundary mismatches produce radiative damping. In the frustrated fivefold tetrahedral cluster, radiation into the continuum is suppressed if the excitation frequency $\omega$ falls within the phononic/polaritonic bandgap:
$$\omega_{\text{lower}} < \omega < \omega_{\text{upper}}$$

## 2. Non-Radiating Dark Modes
Under the bandgap condition, leaked energy cannot propagate to spatial infinity. It is forced into localized, evanescent circulation modes (Dark Modes):
$$\nabla \cdot \mathbf{S}_{\text{rad}} = 0, \quad \mathbf{S}_{\text{reactive}} \neq 0$$

The energy circulates continuously along the 5-tetrahedral closed loop, compensating for the angular deficit strain and acting as a structural shock absorber.
