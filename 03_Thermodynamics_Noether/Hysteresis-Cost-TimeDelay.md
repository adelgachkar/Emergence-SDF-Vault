# Substrate Hysteresis Cost and Causal Time-Delay Bound

$$\Delta\mathcal{E}_{\text{loss}} = \oint_{\mathcal{C}_5} \mathbf{F}_{\text{restoration}} \cdot d\mathbf{x} > 0$$

## 2. Microscopic Time Delay ($\tau_d$)
