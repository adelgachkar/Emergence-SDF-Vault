# Rank-Ladder Glossary

> **Structural Causal Chain:**
> balance differential ΔB (rank-0 scalar) → onset arrow (rank-1 vector) → substrate couplings (rank-2+ tensor) → declarative components (existents-as-expressions) → dynamics (stepped flow) — with energy as both the scalar ledger and the dynamics of the same differential, and measurement as self-referential.

This note registers the **family terminology of the emergence rank ladder**, consolidated from the canonical reference LIMEN-VACUI `10_Reference/Emergence-Balance-Reference` (FA+EN) so that all four family repositories speak one vocabulary. It is a glossary — it defines terms and maps them across repositories; it derives nothing new and makes no empirical claim.

## 1. The canonical ladder — term by term

| Term | Definition (in-model) | Rank | Registered status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Balance differential ΔB** | the level difference between the initial-reference level and the post-boundary potential level; the closure constant deposited by the overflow (LIMEN A2). A rank-0 quantity: one number, no internal structure, no direction. | 0 — scalar | [exact/closure] |
| **Onset arrow** | ΔB made directional: the vectorization of the differential. Potential is inherently forward-oriented; the arrow is *created* by the overflow, never pre-written in a pre-boundary law (LIMEN A3, T2 battery). | 1 — vector | [structural] |
| **Substrate couplings** | when many arrowed sites coexist, differential *gradients* become anisotropic: strain-, wall-, and polarization-like objects that transform with composition of directions. The polarized wall envelope is the tensor-stage of the differential (LIMEN K2/T7; SPUMA K2). | 2+ — tensor | [structural] |
| **Declarative components** | existents-as-expressions: components whose identity is their role in carrying dynamics — rings, cavities, harmonics; objects definable only by what they do. | — | [model-internal] |
| **Dynamics** | the stepped flow driven by the differential: lattice stepping, harmonic exchange, constraint release. Dynamics is the ladder *in motion*, not a new tier (LIMEN T1/T5/T7). | — | [measured in-model] |
| **Energy (scalar ledger)** | the numeric record of how much differential remains unexpressed at a site. Never absolute (revere — S4). | 0 | [structural] |
| **Energy (dynamics)** | the stepping of the same differential. The dynamics is not powered *by* energy; the ledger and the flow are two descriptions of one differential being spent. **No third bookkeeping exists.** | — | [model-internal] |
| **Self-referential measurement** | a constraint-coupling between registers; the instrument is made of the same stepped differentials it reports on. Guarded in-model by Ward invariance (three-narrative, three-decoration). | — | [model-internal] |
| **Registry event (emergence)** | what "emergence" means in the family: not a substance appearing, but the overflow simultaneously creating the silent boundary and ΔB. | — | [structural] |

**Reading rule:** the ladder is *compositional* — rank n+1 adds zero substance, only organization of rank-n differentials. This is why every rank's claim stays falsification-friendly.
