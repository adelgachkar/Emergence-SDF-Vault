# Vacuum-Foam Boundary Dynamics

## 1. Microscopic Boundary Model
The classical continuous vacuum is treated here not as an empty Riemannian manifold, but as an active, fluctuating stochastic medium governed by broken time-reversal symmetry ($\mathcal{T}$-breaking). The boundary layer $\partial \Omega$ separates unorganized stochastic foam fluctuations from bounded, coherent lattice domains.

## 2. Dynamic Impedance Matching
At the boundary layer, the kinetic exchange between vacuum fluctuations and internal modes is governed by the boundary normal flux:
$$J_n = -\hat{\mathbf{n}} \cdot \left[ \kappa \nabla \rho_{\text{foam}} + \mathbf{v}_{\text{drift}} \rho_{\text{foam}} \right]$$

To maintain homeostatic boundaries without runaway energy inflation:
1. High-frequency turbulent components are backscattered into the stochastic sea.
2. Harmonic resonant modes are directed inward toward the minimal tetrahedral units ([[Minimal-Tetrahedral-Unit]]).

## 3. Physical Significance
This boundary condition establishes the non-axiomatic physical anchor: matter and field fields are localized, stabilized topological excitations maintained across the vacuum interface.
